1. PUBLIC MEMBER SELF-SERVICE ONBOARDING PORTAL (STAGE 2)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Portal Access URL:
- Live: http://mors.kimtechconsultancy.co.ke/portal
- Local: http://localhost:5173/portal

Who Uses This:
All 349 Gazetted Members of the National Assembly (290 Constituency MPs, 
47 County Woman Representatives, 12 Nominated Members).

Authentication Method:
No password required. Members search using their name as published in the 
official IEBC Gazette Notice (e.g. "Babu Owino", "Ndindi Nyoro", "Passaris", 
"Gladys Shollei", "Ichung'wah", "Tim Wanyonyi", "Sabina Chege").

Member Capabilities:
- Search and claim their authoritative gazette record.
- Verify prefilled gazette particulars (Name, Category, County, Constituency, Party - Locked).
- Enter personal biodata (Date of Birth, National ID, Phone, Email, KRA PIN, Address, Qualifications, Next of Kin).
- Upload digital scans of National ID/Passport, Form 23 IEBC Certificate, and official passport photo.
- Generate and print an official MORS Reference Slip (e.g., MORS-14P-XXXX).
- Receive automated confirmation email containing Secretariat support contacts.

3. THE 8 STATUTORY REPORTS AVAILABLE IN MORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Access: Available via the "Statutory Reports" menu or at /reports
1. Executive Summary Report: Overall counts, funnel stages, and unit progress.
2. Register of Members Report: Complete roll by category (290 Constituency, 47 Woman Rep, 12 Nominated).
3. Online Submissions Audit Report: Timestamped pre-registrations with document upload statuses.
4. Physical Onboarding Report: Desk check-in records, verifying clerk identity, oath and assets filing.
5. Outstanding Clearance by Unit: Real-time bottleneck report showing Members pending each unit.
6. Demographics & Profile Insights: Statistical analysis of age, gender, youth, and parties.
7. Document Verification Status Report: Audit of National ID, IEBC Certificate, and photo approvals.
8. Comprehensive System Audit Trail: Complete immutable historical log with IP addresses and actions.
