1. PUBLIC MEMBER SELF-SERVICE ONBOARDING PORTAL (STAGE 2)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Portal Access URL:
- Live: http://mors.kimtechconsultancy.co.ke/portal
- Local: http://localhost:5173/portal

Authentication Method:
No password required. Members search using their name as published in the 
official IEBC Gazette Notice (e.g. "Babu Owino", "Ndindi Nyoro", "Passaris", 
"Gladys Shollei", "Ichung'wah", "Tim Wanyonyi", "Sabina Chege").

Member Capabilities & Rules:
1. Email Address:
   - Uses standard/personal Email Address (official parliament emails are not required at this stage since they are allocated upon full onboarding).
2. Personal Assistant Information:
   - Captures designated PA particulars: Full Name, Mobile Phone Number, and Email Address.
3. Document Attachments (Optional):
   - Scanned uploads (Passport Photo, National ID/Passport, IEBC Form 23 Certificate) are OPTIONAL. Members who have not scanned their documents yet can still complete online submission and present physical originals directly at the Registration Desk.
4. NOTICE:
   - Authoritative institutional notice:
     "NOTICE: (Physical verification of documents is mandatory to ensure full onboarding)"
5. Reference Slip:
   - Generates official MORS Reference Slip (e.g., MORS-14P-0042) and queues confirmation notice.
